Need a little clarity?

Questions, answered simply.

A few useful notes about choosing products, building a routine, delivery, and the limits of this portfolio demo.

What order should I use the products in?
Start with Soft Reset Cleanser. Follow with Dew Drop Barrier Serum. Finish your morning routine with Day Shield SPF 40. Introduce one new product at a time and patch-test first.
Can I use the routine if my skin is sensitive?
The demo is designed around gentle, uncomplicated steps, but it is not medical advice. Patch-test new products, stop if irritation occurs, and speak with a qualified professional for persistent concerns.
How does the Flow Guide work?
Flow Guide is a static, offline product helper in this portfolio build. Ask about a product name or include a location, such as “Dew Drop Serum in Lahore,” and it returns simple product information without requiring an AI API.
How does delivery work?
Use the routine request form on the home page to submit your name, location, and chosen product. The demo confirms that your product is on the way. In production, the PHP/MySQL endpoint stores that request for fulfillment.
Can I return a product?
Returns are represented as placeholder policy content in this portfolio demo. A real store should publish a clear return window, eligibility rules, contact route, and refund timing before launch.
Is checkout real?
No. The checkout is a portfolio demonstration. It can create local demo orders through the PHP/MySQL backend, but it does not process payments.
